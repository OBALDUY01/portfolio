About me

I'm a 2D & 3D Motion Designer and 3D Artist based in Kazakhstan, working with brands, apps, and studios that care about how things look and feel in motion.

My work sits at the intersection of design and storytelling — I don't just animate things, I build visual systems that communicate. From app promos and cinematic ads to game development and 3D art, I pick the tool the idea needs, not the one that looks impressive on paper.

Clients have included Kaspi.kz, Welltory, Hulu, Stüssy, and Anixart.

Jana Post
2D Motion Design
Making cross-border commerce feel effortless.
Overview
Jana Post is the first private platform enabling users to buy goods from Chinese manufacturers and track logistics from China to Kazakhstan. The challenge: explain a complex cross-border supply chain in a way that feels simple and trustworthy.
What I did
Designed a clear 2D motion narrative that walks the user through the journey — from product selection in China to delivery in Kazakhstan. Used map-based transitions, clean iconography, and a logical visual flow to make the process feel transparent and easy.
Result
An explainer-style promo that builds immediate trust with a new audience, turning a complex logistics product into something anyone can understand in under 60 seconds.

The first private platform enabling users to purchase goods from Chinese manufacturers and track logistics from China to Kazakhstan.

Welltory
2D/3D Motion Design
Making health data feel human.

Welltory is a smart health app that measures heart rate variability and stress levels. Health data is often cold and clinical — the challenge was to make it feel personal, approachable, and worth paying attention to.

An app promo that turns raw biometric data into a compelling visual story — making a complex product feel immediately understandable and desirable.
